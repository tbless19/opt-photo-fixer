Red, green, blue and brightness change the whole photo evenly, like a camera's white balance. That is the safe kind of adjustment. Whiten background only edits just the pixels that match the wall color. Keep it low, and check that your hair edges and face aren't affected. Heavy background editing can get a photo rejected.
